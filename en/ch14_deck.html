Chapter 14

Deck boards

Deck is Nextcloud's board-style project management, like a self-hosted Trello. By the end of this chapter, you'll be able to use boards, lists and cards to organize what your family or your team has to do, and to give each item an owner and a due date.

Put your work on a board

A to-do written on paper is easy to forget, and to-dos scattered across different places are hard to track. Deck makes the work visible as "board → list → card": at a glance you see what is still to do, what is in progress and what is done. It suits dividing up jobs at home and small team projects.

Chapter task: create a board, lay out its lists, add cards, and set owners and due dates.

Boards, lists and cards

LevelWhat it isExample
BoardOne project or topic"Moving plan"
List (Stack)A stage column inside a boardTo do / In progress / Done
CardA single task"Call the moving company"
A card holds a lot: an assignee, a due date, labels, a description, attachments, a subtask checklist, even a link to a Nextcloud file.

Five steps to start using Deck

  1. Open Deck

    Click "Deck" in the Apps menu.

  2. Create a board

    Click "+" to add a board, name it and pick a color (for example "Moving plan").

  3. Create the lists

    Inside the board, create your lists: To do, In progress, Done.

  4. Add cards

    Add a card to the "To do" list and give it a task name. Click the card to add a due date, an assignee and a description.

  5. Drag to track progress

    When a task starts, drag its card to "In progress"; when it is finished, drag it to "Done". Progress is visible at a glance.

Splitting the work and collaborating

  • Share the board with an account on this site so the whole family or the whole team can use it.
  • Assign an owner on each card so everyone picks up their own tasks.
  • Use labels (colors) to sort tasks by type or by priority.
  • A card can carry a due date, and you get a reminder as it comes up.

Common Deck pitfalls

  1. Deck is nowhere to be found

    Deck is a separate app and may not be enabled. Ask your admin to enable it in the Apps store (Chapter 21).

  2. The person you shared with cannot see the board

    Check that you shared the board with their account and that they have accepted it.

  3. A card will not drag

    Refresh the page. On a phone, press and hold to drag, or move the card from its menu.

  4. No due-date reminder appeared

    Check that your notification settings are on (Chapter 5) and that the card really has a due date.

Deck FAQ

What is the difference between Deck and Tasks?
Deck is board-style (drag cards between columns, which suits projects); Tasks is a list-style to-do app (Chapter 17) that is integrated with your calendar. Use whichever you find more natural.
Is there a limit on how many boards I can have?
There is no hard limit, but one board per project is the advice; too many of them get harder to manage, not easier.
Can a card link to a file?
Yes. A card can have a Nextcloud file or a link attached, which keeps the task and its material in one place.
Can I use it on my phone?
There is a Deck mobile app, or you can work from your phone's browser.
Do finished cards disappear?
No. Drag them to the "Done" list or archive them, and you can look back at them at any time.
