Chapter 15

Office: documents online

Nextcloud Office lets you edit Word / Excel / PowerPoint documents right in your browser, and several people can work on the same file at once. By the end of this chapter, you'll be able to change a document without downloading it, and your edits save straight back to the cloud.

No more downloading and re-uploading documents

The traditional routine is "download → edit → re-upload" — tedious, and prone to version confusion. Nextcloud Office lets you open a document in the browser, edit it and collaborate on it; your changes save back to the original file, so you skip the pain of moving files back and forth.

Chapter task: open a document in Office, edit it online, and try co-editing with someone else.

What Office is and where it comes from

Under the hood, Nextcloud Office is Collabora Online or OnlyOffice (whichever the site has installed). Both are open-source online office engines, and they support .docx, .xlsx and .pptx as well as OpenDocument (.odt/.ods/.odp).

File typeEquivalent
DocumentWord (.docx) / ODF (.odt)
SpreadsheetExcel (.xlsx) / ODF (.ods)
PresentationPowerPoint (.pptx) / ODF (.odp)
Templates are ready to use: Files usually has a /Templates folder (invoices, resumes, schedules and so on) that you can apply when you create a new document.

Five steps to start using Office

  1. Open a document in Files

    Click a .docx or .xlsx file and the editor opens right in your browser.

  2. Create a new document

    In Files, click "+" → New document / spreadsheet / presentation, name it and start editing.

  3. Edit, and let it autosave

    Edit as you would in the desktop client. What you write saves back to the original file, with no manual upload.

  4. Invite people to collaborate

    Share the document with an account on this site (can edit). Everyone can then edit at the same time and see each other's cursors.

  5. Download or export

    If you need an offline copy, use "Download" and pick the original format or convert to PDF.

Real-time collaboration

  • Several people edit at the same time and see each other's cursors and changes.
  • Use comments and track changes to discuss the content.
  • Version history lets you go back (under the file's versions, or in Activity).
Format compatibility: complex Word / Excel formatting can come out slightly differently in an open-source engine. For a document that matters, download it and check the formatting once more.

Common Office pitfalls

  1. Clicking a file does not open the editor

    The Office component (Collabora / OnlyOffice) may not be installed or not started. That is an admin setting (Chapter 21).

  2. It loads for a long time, or stays blank

    The Office engine is fairly resource-hungry, so the first load is slow. It is most noticeable when the server is underpowered.

  3. The formatting shifts

    Complex formatting can differ in an open-source engine. For a document that matters, download it and check it in the original software.

  4. It stalls while you collaborate

    Several people editing at once is demanding on the connection, and a poor network shows up as lag. Cut the number of people editing at the same time.

Office FAQ

Do I have to pay extra?
The community editions of Collabora / OnlyOffice are open source and free of charge; the enterprise editions add extra features. Which one this site runs is your admin's decision.
Can I open Google Docs?
Export the Google document to .docx / .xlsx first and upload it, then open it with Office.
Do my edits save automatically?
Yes. What you edit saves back to the original file, and the version history is kept.
Can I edit on my phone?
You can, but editing on a small screen is limited; do complex documents on a computer.
Do my documents get sent anywhere outside?
No. The Office engine runs on your own server, and documents never leave it.
