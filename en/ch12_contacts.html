Chapter 12

Contacts

Put your address book into Nextcloud: add contacts, sort them into groups, import and export vCard, and sync with your phone. By the end of this chapter, your contacts will all sit at home in one place, and switching phones no longer risks losing them.

Your address book belongs in your own hands

Contacts are both highly personal and important data. Keep them in Nextcloud and they stay at home, sync across devices and can be backed up; when you switch phones they sync straight back, with no dependence on your phone maker or a third-party cloud.

Chapter task: add a contact, create a group, and get to know vCard import and export and phone sync.

Address books, groups and CardDAV

ConceptWhat it means
Address bookA set of contacts; you can keep several
GroupA way to sort your contacts (family, colleagues, suppliers)
vCard (.vcf)The standard exchange format for contacts, used for import and export
CardDAVThe standard protocol for contact sync; it is what your phone syncs through

Five steps to start using Contacts

  1. Open Contacts

    Click "Contacts" in the Apps menu. Your address books and groups are on the left.

  2. Add a contact

    Click "+ New contact" and fill in the fields: name, phone, email, address and so on.

  3. Sort into groups

    Assign a group inside the contact (family, colleagues), or create the group first and file contacts into it.

  4. Import an existing address book

    Export a vCard (.vcf) from your phone or another service, then load it in Contacts with "Import".

  5. Set up phone sync

    Add an account on your phone with the CardDAV URL (you can copy it from the settings), and your contacts stay in two-way sync.

Backing up is easy: at any time you can "Export" a whole address book as a vCard and keep a copy, which helps when you switch phones or move to another service.

Syncing with your phone

  • iOS: Settings → add a CardDAV account, then fill in the server URL and your credentials (an app password is recommended).
  • Android: use an app that supports CardDAV (DAVx5, for example) to add the account and sync.
  • Once it is syncing, contacts you add or change on your phone show up in Nextcloud on their own, and the other way around.

Common contacts pitfalls

  1. Imported names come out as garbage

    That is a vCard encoding problem. Check that the export used UTF-8, or export again with a different tool. Note: any name outside plain ASCII is affected the same way, accented letters included.

  2. The phone will not sync

    Check the CardDAV URL and your credentials (an app password is more reliable), and make sure the app is set up correctly.

  3. Duplicate contacts

    Importing more than once, or syncing from several sources, produces duplicates. Use the de-duplicate feature or merge them by hand.

  4. Contact photos did not sync

    Some fields (the avatar) depend on what the app supports, so not all of them sync.

Contacts FAQ

Can I move my contacts over from Google Contacts?
You can. Export a vCard from Google, then import it in Contacts.
Can I have several address books?
Yes. Keep address books like "Personal" and "Work" apart, and each one can be shared separately.
What is the difference between a group and an address book?
An address book is the top-level division (it can be synced and shared on its own); a group is a sub-division inside an address book (family, colleagues).
Will my contacts come back when I switch phones?
They will. Set up the same CardDAV account on the new phone and your contacts sync back.
Can I share an address book with someone?
Yes. Share the address book with an account on this site, which suits a team that works from one customer list.
