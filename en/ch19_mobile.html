Chapter 19

Mobile app

Install the Nextcloud app on your phone, connect it to your server, and let your photos back up automatically while your files travel with you. By the end of this chapter, the photos on your phone land at home on their own, and the files that matter are readable offline.

Your phone holds the most photos

Your phone is the device you take photos and save files on most often, and the one most easily lost or broken. Install the Nextcloud app and turn on auto upload, and the photos on your phone keep backing up to your server at home; the files you need travel with you as well.

Chapter task: Install the app, log in to your server, turn on auto upload for photos, and learn how offline access works.

Auto upload vs manual

MethodHow it worksBest for
Auto uploadThe app sends new photos to the server in the backgroundDay to day; set it up and forget it
Manual uploadYou pick the files and upload them yourselfCatching up on old files, controlling the timing
Offline accessMark a file as available offlineImportant files you have to read with no network
Background backup needs permissions: iOS / Android restrict what runs in the background, so the app needs the matching permissions (photos, background refresh) before it can back up on its own. Grant them when you first set it up.

Six steps to set up phone backup

  1. Download the app

    Search for "Nextcloud" in the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and install the official app.

  2. Enter the server URL

    Open the app and fill in https://woowtech-nextcloud.woowtech.io (the full address, including https).

  3. Log in and authorize

    Log in with your username and password and authorize the app; we recommend pairing it with an app password (Chapter 5).

  4. Turn on auto upload

    In the app settings turn on "Auto upload", then choose the camera album and the target folder.

  5. Grant the permissions

    Allow access to photos, background refresh and notifications, so the automatic backup runs smoothly.

  6. Confirm the backup has started

    Go back to Files / Photos on the web, and when the photos from your phone start showing up, it is working.

The first backup takes a long time: If the phone already holds a great many photos, the first full backup runs long and eats data. Connect to Wi-Fi before you start, and leave the app in the foreground for a while.

Settings worth adjusting

  • Upload on Wi-Fi only: saves mobile data.
  • Include videos: videos are large, so include them only if you want them.
  • Original quality: check that it uploads the original file and not a compressed version.
  • Offline marking: mark the files you use often as available offline, so they open with no network.

Common app pitfalls

  1. It cannot reach the server

    Check that the URL is complete and includes https, and that your network is up. Open the same URL in the phone browser to test.

  2. Background backup does not run

    Check the photos, background refresh and notification permissions; exclude the app from battery optimization.

  3. The backup stops halfway

    Switch to Wi-Fi, check the battery level, and bring the app to the foreground to carry on. Large videos get stuck easily, so back up the photos first.

  4. Duplicate photos

    Check that auto upload has no duplicate source set; when you change phones, log in with the same account and anything already backed up is not uploaded again.

Mobile app FAQ

Is the data the same in the app and on the web?
It is. They connect to the same server with the same account, and the files are shared between them.
Does it use a lot of mobile data?
It does, especially the first full backup. Set "Upload on Wi-Fi only", or run the first one through on Wi-Fi.
If I delete a photo on my phone, does the server copy go too?
Auto upload is a "backup", so by default deleting a photo on the phone does not delete the server copy. It still depends on your settings, so confirm before you delete.
Does the backup start over when I change phones?
Log in with the same account and that is all it takes. Anything already backed up is not sent again, and new photos keep backing up.
Can I use Calendar and Contacts on my phone too?
You can, over CalDAV / CardDAV sync (Chapters 11 and 12), or with the matching official apps.
