Chapter 13

Talk: chat and video

Talk is the chat and video meeting app built into Nextcloud. By the end of this chapter, you'll be able to open a one-to-one or group conversation, start a video meeting and share your screen, all of it on a server in your own home.

Video meetings on your own server

Talking to family and colleagues in real time usually means reaching for a third-party messaging app. Talk lets you chat, hold meetings and share your screen inside your own Nextcloud, so messages and call data stay at home and privacy stays in your hands.

Chapter task: open a conversation, send a message, start a video call, and get to know screen sharing.

Conversations, calls and files

FeatureWhat it means
One-to-one conversationPrivate messages with a single user
Group conversationA chat room for several people; you can name it and add members
Video / audio callStarted from inside a conversation; supports several people and screen sharing
Public conversation linkGenerates a link that lets outside guests join the meeting
Integrated with your files: you can share a Nextcloud file straight into a conversation; the other person clicks and it opens, with nothing to send separately.

Five steps to start using Talk

  1. Open Talk

    Click "Talk" in the Apps menu. Your conversations are listed on the left.

  2. Start a new conversation

    Click "+" to create a conversation, then pick a single user (one-to-one) or set up a group chat room.

  3. Send a message

    Type in the box at the bottom and send it; you can attach a file or mention (@) someone.

  4. Start a video call

    Click the call icon at the top right of the conversation to start audio or video; other members can join.

  5. Share your screen

    During a call, click the screen sharing icon and choose your whole screen or a single window. It suits presentations and teaching.

Video needs bandwidth: video with several people is hard on the network. When there are a lot of participants, or the quality is poor, turn the video off and keep audio only, or ask your admin to set up a TURN server to improve the connection.

Inviting outside guests

  • In the conversation settings, generate a "public conversation link" and send it to people who have no account.
  • You can set a password to protect the meeting link.
  • Disable the link once the meeting is over, so nobody can come in later.
A meeting link is a key too: if a public meeting link leaks, a stranger can join. Set a password, and disable it the moment the meeting is over.

Common Talk pitfalls

  1. The call will not connect, or it spins forever

    Usually this is a NAT traversal problem, and your admin needs to set up a TURN server. First try the same network segment, or switch to audio.

  2. The other person cannot hear me

    Check the browser's microphone permission, and confirm that the right input device is selected.

  3. Screen sharing shows nothing

    The browser has to be granted screen sharing permission; some browsers also need it allowed in your system settings.

  4. Talk is nowhere to be found

    Talk is a separate app and may not be enabled yet. Ask your admin to enable it in the Apps store (Chapter 21).

Talk FAQ

Where are messages stored?
On your own Nextcloud server, without passing through a third-party messaging platform.
Can I use Talk on my phone?
You can. There is a Nextcloud Talk mobile app that receives messages and takes calls.
How many people can join a video call?
It depends on your server's performance and on whether you have TURN / SFU. For a small home setup, keep the numbers down.
Can I record a meeting?
Some versions and settings support recording. It needs extra components, it is on the advanced side, and your admin sets it up.
Can outside guests see my other data?
No. A guest can only enter the conversation or meeting you shared, and cannot see any of your other files.
