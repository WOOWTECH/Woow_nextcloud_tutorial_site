Chapter 17

Tasks

Tasks is Nextcloud's to-do list. It is integrated with Calendar and syncs across your devices. By the end of this chapter, you'll be able to create tasks, set due dates and priorities, and make your tasks appear in your calendar.

Run your own to-do list

Keep your to-dos in an ordinary app and they scatter the moment you switch devices. Tasks keeps them in Nextcloud, on the same data Calendar uses, lets you set due dates and priorities, and syncs them across your devices, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Chapter task: create a list and a task in it, set a due date and a priority, and understand how Tasks integrates with Calendar.

Tasks and Calendar are the same thing underneath

Tasks and Calendar share CalDAV underneath: a "list" is really a calendar. That is why your tasks sync alongside your calendar and show up in it.

ConceptWhat it means
ListA set of tasks, backed by one calendar
TaskA single item, which can carry a due date, a priority and subtasks
Calendar integrationA task with a due date shows up in Calendar

Five steps to start using Tasks

  1. Open Tasks

    Click "Tasks" in the Apps menu. The left-hand list holds your task lists.

  2. Create a list

    Add a list (for example "Chores" or "Work") and pick a color to tell them apart.

  3. Add a task

    Type a task name in the list and press Enter; click into it to fill in the details.

  4. Set a due date and a priority

    Inside a task, set the due date and the priority (high / medium / low). You can add subtasks and notes as well.

  5. Check it off

    Check a task off when it is done and it moves to Completed, where you can review it or restore it whenever you need to.

Linked to your calendar: a task with a due date shows up in Calendar, in the calendar that matches its list, so a single glance tells you what needs doing today.

Syncing across devices

  • Add an account in the to-do or calendar app on your phone using the CalDAV URL, and your tasks sync both ways.
  • On Android, use a to-do app that supports CalDAV; on iOS, Reminders can integrate over CalDAV too.
  • On the desktop it syncs together with Calendar, so there is nothing separate to set up.

Common Tasks pitfalls

  1. Tasks is nowhere to be found

    Tasks is a separate app and may not be enabled. Ask your admin to enable it (Chapter 21).

  2. A task does not show up in your calendar

    Check that the task has a due date; one without a due date does not appear on the calendar timeline.

  3. Your phone will not sync

    Check the CalDAV account settings (an app password is recommended) and confirm they point at the right list.

  4. Subtasks will not expand

    Click a task to open its detail view and see the subtasks; the list view may show only the top-level tasks.

Tasks FAQ

Should I use Tasks or Deck?
Tasks suits personal, list-style to-dos and integrates with Calendar; Deck suits board-style project collaboration (Chapter 14). You can use both.
Can I share a task?
Yes. Share the list it belongs to (the calendar) with an account on this site, which works well for a household chore list.
Can it sync with Reminders on iOS?
It can, over CalDAV. The setup is on the advanced side, so go by the official docs.
Are completed tasks deleted?
No. They move to Completed, where you can review or restore them.
Can I set a recurring task?
Yes, you can set a recurrence rule (daily / weekly), which works well for regular chores.
