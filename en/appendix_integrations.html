Appendix C

External integrations

Nextcloud is not an island. This appendix maps out how it connects to Home Assistant, to backup tools and to other services, so your cloud becomes one part of the digital infrastructure at home.

Wire your cloud into home automation

Nextcloud has open APIs (WebDAV, OCS) and standard protocols (CalDAV/CardDAV), so it can connect to Home Assistant, to backup tools and to scripts. Integrate it well and files, calendars and notifications all move on their own.

This appendix: it covers the integration options and what to watch out for, not step-by-step install instructions; how you actually connect depends on the tools you use.

Connecting to Home Assistant

IntegrationWhat you can do with it
Calendar integrationBring Nextcloud Calendar (CalDAV) into HA and trigger automations from events
Files / WebDAVKeep the backups, snapshots and logs HA produces in Nextcloud
NotificationsUse Nextcloud Talk as HA's notification channel (where the integration supports it)
Both on one box: in this site's setup Nextcloud and HA are on the same machine, so they reach each other faster internally; both go out through Cloudflare Tunnel, so neither has to open a port of its own.

Backup and sync tools

  • rclone: treat Nextcloud (WebDAV) as a remote and sync it to other storage on a schedule (an off-site backup), or pull outside data in the other direction.
  • HA snapshots / backups: in an add-on setup you can pair this with HA's own backup mechanism and protect settings and data together.
  • The 3-2-1 rule: at least three copies, two kinds of media, one of them off-site. A self-hosted cloud needs a second layer of backup as well.
Nextcloud is not a backup: it is your primary storage, and you still have to back it up somewhere else — that is what stands between you and a dead disk or an accidental delete.

APIs and automation

InterfaceWhat it is for
WebDAVReading and writing files, mounting, moving files from a script (Chapter 20)
OCS APIAdmin operations such as users, shares and capabilities
CalDAV/CardDAVCalendar and contact sync (Chapters 11 and 12)
Use an app password: use an app password (Chapter 5) for every automated connection, and never write your main password into a script or a config file.

Common integration pitfalls

  1. HA cannot reach the calendar

    Check the CalDAV URL, the account and the app password; the time zones have to match.

  2. rclone will not connect

    Use the WebDAV URL (including the account path) and an app password; check that the https certificate is valid.

  3. An automation never fires

    Test by hand first that the API responds, then check the automation's conditions and its permissions.

  4. Not enough permissions

    Some operations need admin or a specific permission; use the right account and token.

Integrations FAQ

Do I have to know how to code before I can integrate anything?
Not necessarily. Calendar and contact sync, and rclone backups, are mostly a matter of configuration; only deeper automation takes a bit of scripting.
Do integrations bring a security risk?
They do, which is why you always use an app password, go over HTTPS, grant least privilege, and check your connected devices regularly.
Can I treat Nextcloud as my only backup?
We do not recommend it. It is your primary storage, and you still need a second, off-site layer with something like rclone.
Should HA and Nextcloud be on two separate machines?
It depends on your resources. One machine is less work and the link between them is faster; separate machines keep them from affecting each other and are easier to grow. Decide by what you need.
Where is the OCS API documentation?
In the Nextcloud developer documentation, which covers endpoints for shares, users, capabilities and more.
